About

A personal workshop, kept in the open

Brokild Apps is a personal side project — separate from any work or research page. Everything here runs entirely in your browser: no server, no accounts, and nothing is uploaded. Most of these started as a small itch to scratch, and they stay online in case they scratch someone else’s too.

Everything is open source. If something is broken or you have an idea, the repository is the place to say so.

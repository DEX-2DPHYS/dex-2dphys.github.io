Limitations
  • Position is measured in pixels, not meters.
  • Camera motion looks like pendulum motion — hold the phone still or use a support.
  • Lighting changes may disturb colour tracking.
  • Velocity and especially acceleration amplify camera noise.
  • Camera frame rate limits temporal resolution.
  • This is a simple educational measurement tool, not a calibrated laboratory instrument.

Scientific papers, without the codes

DTU Science Writer is a writing program for papers and proposals. What you see is the paper: equations, numbered figures and tables, cross-references, citations and a reference list in the journal's style. The page preview beside the text is the exact PDF. Co-authors who work in Word or LaTeX get real files, and their versions come back in.

What it does

Writing, as it will print

Equations in the text and on their own line, figures placed at the top of a page, in the text or with the text around them, tables, footnotes, contents lists. Cross-references such as “Fig. 3” and “Eq. (2)” follow when things move.

References that come out right

Cite from your own list, from Zotero, or by pasting a DOI or arXiv number. Pick the journal and the reference style, section numbering and line spacing follow; article numbers, journal abbreviations and title case are put right for you.

Made for co-authors

Comments and track changes; a shared folder where one person edits and the others read and comment. A real Word file out, with equations, captions, comments and revisions, and Word files in. LaTeX for Overleaf. The Supporting Information as a second tab of the same paper.

Coming from Word

An existing manuscript comes in as it is. Import a Word file and its equations, figures with captions, tables, footnotes, comments and tracked changes become the real thing here. References typed as “[1]” or as superscript numbers are looked up one by one and become real citations; typed “Fig. 2” becomes a live reference. A report lists what arrived and what to check.

And it goes back out. The Word export is an ordinary Word document: Word's own equations, numbered captions, cross-references that update, comments with replies, tracked changes, and Zotero citations that a co-author with Zotero can keep using. A guide (F1) and a short tutorial paper are built in.

Install

Download DTUScienceWriter-setup.exe and run it. It installs for your own user in a few seconds, with no admin rights, adds DTU Science Writer to the Start menu and the desktop, and opens .dtusw files.

Windows will warn you the first time. The program is not code-signed, so Windows SmartScreen shows "Windows protected your PC". Click More info, then Run anyway. This happens once.

Open Help → Tutorial for a ten-minute tour in a paper you can practise in.

Your papers and your computer

Your papers stay yours. A paper is one file on your own disk, with its figures, references, comments and changes inside. Earlier saves are kept as backups, unsaved work is offered again after a crash, and a save never overwrites a newer version made by someone else.

What goes over the internet is only what you ask for: looking up a DOI or a reference, your own Zotero library if you connect it, a journal's reference style, and the check for a new version. Nothing of your text is sent anywhere.

The program reads and writes only where you point it: the files you open, save or export. It is open source under the MIT licence, so anyone can read what it does. A new version is installed only when it carries the program's own signature; the portable version tells you when there is a new one to download.

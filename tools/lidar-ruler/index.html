Checking augmented-reality support…

No depth sensor access in this browser

Full AR measuring needs WebXR augmented reality, which iPhone Safari doesn't yet offer — Apple doesn't expose LiDAR to web pages. On Android, Chrome on an ARCore phone measures with the real depth system.

Here the app uses a tilt estimate instead: aim the ring at the point where an object meets the floor, and the distance is triangulated from the phone's tilt and its height above the floor (adjustable in the app). It's a good estimate, not a LiDAR measurement.

Everything runs on the device — the camera image and depth data never leave your phone.

Model assumptions

Semiclassical model: no Dirac-equation solution, quantum interference, weak localization, universal conductance fluctuations, tunnelling, or edge-state quantization. FEM is a local drift–diffusion model. Trajectories are semiclassical and phase-incoherent. The crossover adds Sharvin and Drude resistances in series.
